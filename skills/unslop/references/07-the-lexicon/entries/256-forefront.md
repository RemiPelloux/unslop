#### 256. `forefront`

- Kind: noun. Banned forms: `forefront`. The phrase `at the forefront` has its own entry.
- Why it reads as machine text: `at the forefront of` is a stock claim of leadership that models attach to any company on an about page. It asserts rank without a source or a comparison. The word also appears in `bring to the forefront`, a padded way to say "make visible".
- Where it shows up: about pages, press releases, research center descriptions.

```slop
We are at the forefront of sustainable fashion.
The pandemic brought remote work to the forefront.
Our lab is at the forefront of quantum research.
```

Replace with:

- The evidence: what you did first, papers, patents, market share.
- "led" or "first", with the date.
- "made common" or "made visible" for the "bring to" form.
- Cut it when there is no evidence of rank.

Rewrites:

```slop
We are at the forefront of sustainable fashion.
```

After: Since 2021, 80% of our fabric has been recycled cotton or deadstock.

```slop
The pandemic brought remote work to the forefront.
```

After: In 2020, the share of our staff working from home went from 5% to 90%.

```slop
Our lab is at the forefront of quantum research.
```

After: The lab has published 40 papers on error correction since 2019 and runs a 72-qubit device.

- Literal use: for the front of a group or object, write "front".
