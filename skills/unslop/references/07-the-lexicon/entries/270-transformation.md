#### 270. `transformation`

- Kind: noun. Banned forms: `transformation`, `transformations`. The adjective `transformative` has its own entry.
- Why it reads as machine text: `transformation` is consulting vocabulary (`digital transformation`, `business transformation`) that models use for any change larger than a bug fix. It claims a total change of form while hiding what changed and by how much. Most projects given the label are migrations or reorganizations.
- Where it shows up: consulting pages, annual reports, case studies.

```slop
We guide enterprises through digital transformation.
The redesign was a complete transformation of the user experience.
Our transformation journey began in 2020.
```

Replace with:

- "migration", "rewrite", "redesign" or "reorganization", whichever it was.
- Before and after numbers.
- "change" when a plain noun is enough.
- Cut it when the sentence only announces change; list the changes.

Rewrites:

```slop
We guide enterprises through digital transformation.
```

After: We move companies off on-premise ERP systems to cloud accounting, usually in 6 to 9 months.

```slop
The redesign was a complete transformation of the user experience.
```

After: The redesign cut checkout from 5 screens to 2 and halved the number of support tickets about payment.

```slop
Our transformation journey began in 2020.
```

After: In 2020 we moved our 30 internal apps to one cloud provider and closed both data centers.

- Literal use: for data, geometry or CSS, name the operation (`transform: rotate(45deg)`, "ETL step", "matrix multiplication") or put `transformation` in inline code.
