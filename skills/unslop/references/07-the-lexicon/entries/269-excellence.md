#### 269. `excellence`

- Kind: noun. Banned forms: `excellence`.
- Why it reads as machine text: `excellence` is the abstract noun on every values page (`committed to excellence`, `center of excellence`). Models produce it whenever asked for company values or a mission statement. It claims quality without a standard to measure it against, so no reader can check it.
- Where it shows up: values pages, award names, internal team names.

```slop
We are committed to excellence in everything we do.
Our Center of Excellence drives best practices across the org.
Excellence is not an act but a habit.
```

Replace with:

- The standard: "every PR gets two reviews", "99.9% uptime".
- "quality", with a measure next to it.
- For team names, what the team does: "the platform team", "the design systems group".
- Cut it when the sentence is a value with no standard.

Rewrites:

```slop
We are committed to excellence in everything we do.
```

After: Every change gets two reviews, and we publish our uptime and incident reports.

```slop
Our Center of Excellence drives best practices across the org.
```

After: The platform team writes the shared CI templates and reviews each new service before launch.

```slop
Excellence is not an act but a habit.
```

After: Delete the quote; it is Will Durant's paraphrase of Aristotle, and it says nothing about your team.

- Literal use: for an award or institution with the word in its name, put the name in inline code.
