#### 261. `blueprint`

- Kind: noun. Banned forms: `blueprint`, `blueprints`.
- Why it reads as machine text: Models put the label `blueprint` on any plan, template or guide, borrowing the authority of architects' drawings. `a blueprint for success` is a fixed model phrase. It hides whether the reader is getting a checklist, a template or an essay.
- Where it shows up: guide titles, strategy docs, template marketplaces.

```slop
Download our blueprint for scaling your SaaS to $10M ARR.
This article provides a blueprint for building accessible forms.
Our 2025 roadmap is a blueprint for growth.
```

Replace with:

- "plan", "template", "checklist" or "guide", whichever it is.
- "spec" or "design doc" in engineering.
- The steps themselves.
- Cut it when the title can name the content.

Rewrites:

```slop
Download our blueprint for scaling your SaaS to $10M ARR.
```

After: Download the checklist of 12 steps we took from $1M to $10M ARR.

```slop
This article provides a blueprint for building accessible forms.
```

After: This article gives a form template with labels, error text and a tested focus order.

```slop
Our 2025 roadmap is a blueprint for growth.
```

After: The 2025 roadmap has 4 projects, each with a target and an owner.

- Literal use: for an architect's print, write "drawing" or "floor plan"; for Unreal Engine, write `Blueprint` in inline code.
