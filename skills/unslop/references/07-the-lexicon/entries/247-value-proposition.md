#### 247. `value proposition`

- Kind: noun phrase. Banned forms: `value proposition`, `value propositions`, `value prop`.
- Why it reads as machine text: `value proposition` is MBA vocabulary that models bring into every piece of landing-page and pitch advice. Writers then talk about the term instead of stating the offer. The term belongs in an internal planning doc at most; in text a customer reads, it is a sign the actual offer was never written down.
- Where it shows up: pitch decks, landing page briefs, product marketing docs.

```slop
Our value proposition is simple: we save you time.
The new pricing strengthens our value proposition for small teams.
Make sure your hero section communicates a clear value proposition.
```

Replace with:

- The offer: what the customer gets, at what price, in how much time.
- "why customers buy" in internal docs, followed by the reasons heard on sales calls.
- "offer" or "pitch" when you need a noun.
- Cut it when the next sentence states the offer.

Rewrites:

```slop
Our value proposition is simple: we save you time.
```

After: Payroll for 10 people takes 5 minutes a month and costs $8 per person.

```slop
The new pricing strengthens our value proposition for small teams.
```

After: Teams under 10 people now pay nothing for the first year.

```slop
Make sure your hero section communicates a clear value proposition.
```

After: Make the hero headline say what the product does and for whom, such as "Payroll for restaurants, filed in 5 minutes".

- Literal use: in a marketing framework document that defines the term, put `value proposition` in inline code.
