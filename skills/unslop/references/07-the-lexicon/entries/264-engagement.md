#### 264. `engagement`

- Kind: noun. Banned forms: `engagement`, `engagements`. The adjective `engaging` has its own entry.
- Why it reads as machine text: `engagement` is the metric word models use for any user activity, and `drive engagement` is a top phrase in product and marketing text. It hides what users actually do: click, reply, return, pay. It also lets teams count attention-grabbing features as success.
- Where it shows up: product metric docs, social media reports, HR surveys (`employee engagement`).

```slop
The redesign increased engagement by 40%.
Push notifications are a great way to boost engagement.
We measure employee engagement every quarter.
```

Replace with:

- The action counted: "daily active users", "replies per post", "sessions per week".
- "use" or "activity" in a general sense, with the metric next to it.
- For staff surveys, the questions or the score.
- Cut it when there is no metric; the sentence is a slogan.

Rewrites:

```slop
The redesign increased engagement by 40%.
```

After: After the redesign, the share of users who return within 7 days rose from 30% to 42%.

```slop
Push notifications are a great way to boost engagement.
```

After: Users who turn on the weekly digest email open the app on 2 more days a month.

```slop
We measure employee engagement every quarter.
```

After: Each quarter, staff rate 12 statements such as "I know what is expected of me" on a 1 to 5 scale.

- Literal use: for a betrothal, write "engaged" or "betrothal"; for a speaking slot, write "booking"; for combat, write "battle"; for gears, put `engagement` in inline code.
