#### 255. `essence`

- Kind: noun. Banned forms: `essence`, `essences`.
- Why it reads as machine text: Models write `the essence of X` or `capture the essence` to sound as if they reduced something to its center without doing the reduction. It appears in summaries and in brand copy for products and places. It hides the actual property, which is what the reader came for.
- Where it shows up: brand and travel copy, summaries, design critique.

```slop
Our logo captures the essence of the brand.
The essence of good design is simplicity.
This dish captures the essence of Tuscan cooking.
```

Replace with:

- The property: "the logo is a single line drawing of a bridge".
- "main point" or "core idea" when summarizing, then the idea itself.
- The features that make it typical: ingredients, methods, materials.
- Cut it when the next sentence states the property; start there.

Rewrites:

```slop
Our logo captures the essence of the brand.
```

After: The logo is a single-weight line drawing of the Tyne Bridge, set beside the name in IBM Plex Sans.

```slop
The essence of good design is simplicity.
```

After: A good form asks only for the data the next step needs; ours went from 14 fields to 6.

```slop
This dish captures the essence of Tuscan cooking.
```

After: The soup uses stale bread, white beans, kale and olive oil, as Tuscan farm cooks made it.

- Literal use: for a plant oil or flavoring, write "extract" or "oil" ("vanilla extract", "rose oil").
