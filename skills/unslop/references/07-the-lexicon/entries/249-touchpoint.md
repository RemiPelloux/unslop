#### 249. `touchpoint`

- Kind: noun. Banned forms: `touchpoint`, `touchpoints`, `touch point`.
- Why it reads as machine text: `touchpoint` comes from customer-experience consulting, where it means any moment a customer meets the brand. Models use it to make a list of emails, screens and calls sound like a planned system. It hides which channel and which moment, which are exactly what a designer or writer needs to know.
- Where it shows up: CX strategy docs, marketing plans, customer flow maps.

```slop
We optimize every touchpoint across the customer journey.
Email remains a key touchpoint for retention.
Each touchpoint should reflect our brand values.
```

Replace with:

- The channel and the moment: "the welcome email", "the cancel screen", "the renewal call".
- "step" for a flow, numbered.
- "contact" for support conversations.
- Cut it when the list of screens or emails already exists.

Rewrites:

```slop
We optimize every touchpoint across the customer journey.
```

After: We tested the signup page, the welcome email and the first-invoice screen, and rewrote the copy on all three.

```slop
Email remains a key touchpoint for retention.
```

After: The renewal reminder, sent 14 days before the term ends, brings back 1 in 5 lapsed customers.

```slop
Each touchpoint should reflect our brand values.
```

After: The receipt, the shipping email and the return label use the same typeface, logo and tone.

- Literal use: the word has no literal sense in product work; name the screen, email or call.
