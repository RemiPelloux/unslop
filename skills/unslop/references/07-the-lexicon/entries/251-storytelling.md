#### 251. `storytelling`

- Kind: noun. Banned forms: `storytelling`, `storyteller`, `storytellers`.
- Why it reads as machine text: Models apply `storytelling` to any communication with a beginning and an end: `data storytelling`, `brand storytelling`, `visual storytelling`. It promises emotional pull and hides the plain task, which is usually "explain this chart" or "write the about page". It turns ordinary writing advice into a branded skill.
- Where it shows up: data visualization advice, brand guidelines, design conference talks.

```slop
Great dashboards are all about data storytelling.
Our brand storytelling brings our values to life.
Use visual storytelling to guide users through onboarding.
```

Replace with:

- "explain", "show" or "write", plus the object.
- For charts, the one finding the chart must show, placed in its title.
- "sequence" or "order of steps" for onboarding.
- Cut it when the method described is just writing.

Rewrites:

```slop
Great dashboards are all about data storytelling.
```

After: Give each dashboard chart a title that states its finding, such as "Refunds doubled after the March price change".

```slop
Our brand storytelling brings our values to life.
```

After: The about page says who founded the company, why, and what it has made since 2015.

```slop
Use visual storytelling to guide users through onboarding.
```

After: Onboarding shows one screen per step, 4 steps in total, each with one illustration and one sentence.

- Literal use: for the oral craft or performance, write "telling stories" or put `storytelling` in inline code.
