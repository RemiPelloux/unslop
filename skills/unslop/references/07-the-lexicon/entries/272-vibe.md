#### 272. `vibe`

- Kind: noun and verb. Banned forms: `vibe`, `vibes`, `vibed`, `vibing`, `vibey`, and the phrases `vibe coding`, `the vibe is`, `good vibes`, `vibe check`.
- Why it reads as machine text: `vibe` moved from social media slang into model output, and it now fills design briefs, product copy and posts about code. It names a mood instead of a property, so a designer cannot build from it and a reviewer cannot check it. `vibe coding` hides whether anyone read, ran or tested the code.
- Where it shows up: design briefs and moodboards, landing page and office copy, posts about AI-written code.

```slop
The vibe is minimal but warm, like a Scandinavian coffee shop.
I vibe coded the whole backend over a weekend.
Our office has great vibes and a ping-pong table.
```

Replace with:

- The properties: palette hex values, typeface, spacing, corner radius.
- "mood" or "tone" in a brief, followed by those specifics.
- For code, how it was made and checked: "generated with an assistant, not reviewed, no tests".
- Cut it when the sentence describes only a feeling.

Rewrites:

```slop
The vibe is minimal but warm, like a Scandinavian coffee shop.
```

After: Use `#F7F5F0` for the page, one serif for headings, 2px corners, light oak photos, and no shadows.

```slop
I vibe coded the whole backend over a weekend.
```

After: I generated the backend with a coding assistant over a weekend; it has no tests yet and nobody has reviewed it.

```slop
Our office has great vibes and a ping-pong table.
```

After: The office has a quiet floor, lunch on Fridays, and a ping-pong table.

- Literal use: for the instrument in music writing, write "vibraphone"; the word has no other literal sense.
