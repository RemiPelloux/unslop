#### 260. `arsenal`

- Kind: noun. Banned forms: `arsenal`, `arsenals`.
- Why it reads as machine text: Models label a set of tools, features or skills `arsenal` to make it sound strong and ready. It pulls in a military register that almost no product needs. It hides the count and the contents, and it often comes right before a vague list.
- Where it shows up: tool roundups, feature pages, career advice.

```slop
Add this tool to your design arsenal.
Our platform gives marketers a full arsenal of analytics features.
A good debugger is the most important weapon in your arsenal.
```

Replace with:

- "toolkit" or "set of tools", only with the list.
- The tools, named, with what each does.
- "features", with a count.
- Cut it when the noun before it already says what the thing is.

Rewrites:

```slop
Add this tool to your design arsenal.
```

After: This plugin exports Figma frames as SVG files named to match your component files.

```slop
Our platform gives marketers a full arsenal of analytics features.
```

After: Marketers get funnels, cohort charts and A/B test results on one page.

```slop
A good debugger is the most important weapon in your arsenal.
```

After: Learn to set breakpoints and step through code; it finds most bugs faster than print statements.

- Literal use: for a weapons store, write "armory"; for the football club, write `Arsenal` in inline code.
